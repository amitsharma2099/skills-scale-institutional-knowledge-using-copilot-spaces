# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers advocate for the end-user experience and accessibility. They collaborate with product and development teams on usability goals, interaction design, and ensure features are intuitive and inclusive.

### Responsibilities
- Design wireframes, mockups, and prototypes based on user research and requirements
- Conduct usability testing and gather user feedback
- Specify UI/UX acceptance criteria aligned with design standards
- Ensure accessibility standards (WCAG compliance, keyboard navigation, screen readers)
- Participate in design reviews and iterate based on feedback
- Document design specifications and component libraries

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce support burden through better UX
- Build consistent design patterns and brand alignment

### Typical Communication
- Design critique sessions with product and engineering
- Usability test reports and findings
- Design specification documents and Figma links
- Accessibility audit results and recommendations

### Interacts With
- **Product Managers** — understand requirements and user research
- **Developers** — review implementation and component specifications
- **QA** — validate usability acceptance criteria

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers maintain and optimize the build, deploy, and monitoring pipelines. They ensure system resilience, automate infrastructure, and enable rapid, safe releases.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Monitor deployments, system health, and performance metrics
- Implement infrastructure as code and environment automation
- Manage secrets, access controls, and security practices
- Support incident response and post-mortems
- Champion reliability, observability, and best practices

### Goals
- Enable rapid, safe feature delivery
- Reduce deployment lead time and failure rates
- Maintain high system reliability and uptime

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness checklists and release notes
- Incident response and runbooks
- Performance dashboards and alerting
- Infrastructure and automation documentation

### Interacts With
- **Developers** — integrate code into build pipelines, troubleshoot failures
- **QA** — coordinate staging deployments and quality gates
- **Project Managers** — provide release timing and deployment windows

---

## Data Analyst/Scientist

### Role Summary
Data Analysts and Scientists drive data-informed decisions by creating analytics, dashboards, and insights. They measure product success, analyze experiments, and surface actionable metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Define key success metrics and KPIs aligned with business goals
- Build dashboards and reports for product, business, and operational decisions
- Analyze A/B tests, experiments, and user behavior
- Generate insights and actionable recommendations
- Ensure event logging and data quality standards
- Support incident investigation with data analysis

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making
- Measure product impact and ROI
- Identify optimization opportunities through analysis

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and success criteria
- Weekly/monthly dashboard and insight reports
- Experiment analysis and recommendations
- Data quality and instrumentation documentation

### Interacts With
- **Product Managers** — define metrics, analyze feature impact
- **Developers** — specify event logging and instrumentation
- **Stakeholders** — present dashboards and impact demos

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure features and releases follow security best practices, compliance standards, and risk management policies. They are guardians of system and data security.

### Responsibilities
- Perform threat modeling and security architecture reviews
- Approve or flag security-sensitive releases and changes
- Conduct code and infrastructure security reviews
- Define and audit security policies and access controls
- Lead security incident response and post-mortems
- Champion security awareness and secure coding practices

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data exposure
- Ensure compliance with relevant standards (SOC 2, GDPR, etc.)
- Build a security-conscious engineering culture

### Typical Communication
- Security review checklists and approval gates
- Threat models and risk assessments
- Security incident playbooks and notifications
- Security training and policy documentation

### Interacts With
- **Developers** — review code, guide secure coding practices
- **QA** — design security test cases and penetration tests
- **Product/Project Leads** — review risk and approve release gates

---

## Stakeholder Advisor

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Advisors provide consultative input and feedback from business, customer, regulatory, or executive perspectives. They are not day-to-day team members but provide critical alignment and approval at key milestones.

### Responsibilities
- Review project milestones and deliverables for alignment with business goals
- Approve or flag risks with business, customer, or regulatory impact
- Provide executive or customer feedback on scope and priorities
- Escalate concerns and ensure stakeholder alignment
- Participate in key project gates (initiation, release, closure)

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes align with business strategy
- Mitigate business and compliance risks
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and alignment

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and approval gates
- Executive status reports and risk escalations
- Customer feedback sessions and validation meetings
- Project charter and scope approval

### Interacts With
- **Product Managers** — align on vision, priorities, and success metrics
- **Project Managers** — provide status updates, risk review, and approvals
- **All team roles** — as needed for customer/business validation

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When planning projects, identify which personas will participate and clarify their touchpoints and handoffs.
